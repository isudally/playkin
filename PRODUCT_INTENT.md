# PLAYKIN — Product Vision (Authoritative)

**Product category:** Personalized interactive gaming-gift platform  
**Primary occasion:** Birthdays, with other gifting occasions as later or parallel opportunities  
**Status:** Product direction, not a declaration that all features are implemented

## The promise

**Create a game. Make someone's day magical.**

Playkin lets an everyday customer choose a game, personalize it for a loved one, and send a unique playable gift link. The recipient enters a beautiful interactive world created for them, explores and progresses through meaningful gameplay, discovers surprises, and unlocks a personalized gift reveal.

The aim is to make giving a playable game feel as special as giving a thoughtfully chosen physical present.

## This is a platform — not one game

**Playkin is not Dodo's Lantern Trail.** Dodo's Lantern Trail is Game #1, a working technical and creative foundation. It demonstrates creation, delivery, gameplay, replay and reveal, but does not define the limit of the product.

The long-term platform supports **multiple, genuinely different game experiences** with distinct mechanics, worlds, art directions and age-appropriate challenges. Games may include adventures, puzzles, skill/reaction play, explorations, creative challenges or educational experiences. Those are potential families rather than a claim of a released catalogue or a commitment to implement every category.

Dodo may be a recognizable recurring companion without forcing the same character or scene into every game.

## The customer journeys

### Gift-giver / creator

1. Arrive at Playkin and understand immediately what can be gifted.
2. Explore available **real, playable games**, choosing a game type and theme that suit the recipient.
3. Personalize the game with relevant details such as recipient name, occasion, sender identity and message. Future configurations may include age range, interests, difficulty, preferences and optionally responsibly handled media.
4. Preview the actual gifting experience before purchase or delivery.
5. Complete the applicable commercial steps when the payment product is introduced.
6. Send a unique recipient link through WhatsApp or another chosen channel.
7. Manage an issued gift and see appropriate open/interact/completion information with privacy controls.

### Recipient

1. Open a private link on a phone or browser, without confusing onboarding or product administration.
2. Experience a distinctive, visually magical opening personalized for them.
3. Play a **genuine game**, not just read a digital card or tap obvious answers.
4. Progress through challenges, changes in scenery, feedback, collectibles or meaningful outcomes suited to that game.
5. Discover a delightful personalized final reveal.
6. Replay, explore variations or optionally create a gift for someone else.

The playable gift—not a technical dashboard—is the product's emotional center.

## Non-negotiable product standards

### 1. Exceptional art

Every commercial game should feel professionally illustrated, coherent and distinctive. Expressive characters, high-quality game environments, animation, lighting, visual effects and audio are part of the core product. Mismatched stock graphics, shallow templates and low-quality placeholder visuals do not meet the aspiration.

### 2. Engaging gameplay and replay

Different games must be fun for different reasons. Challenges, progression, discoveries and replay variation should be substantive. The goal is **high engagement through enjoyable design**, not manipulative “addiction” mechanics, deceptive rewards or dark patterns, particularly with children.

### 3. Meaningful personalization

Personalization should influence the world, story, content, challenge or ending—not merely replace a heading with the recipient's name. Capabilities for age, interests and optional photos are **future product intentions until they are demonstrably implemented**.

### 4. Magic from beginning to end

Selecting a gift, personalizing it, handing it over, opening it, playing it and reaching the finale should all convey anticipation, delight and polish. A technically functioning form-and-link flow alone is insufficient.

### 5. Natural viral potential

Create moments people **choose** to show friends and family and a natural invitation to give someone else a Playkin gift. Respect recipient control, child privacy and consent. Do not expose identifying names, messages or photos in public previews or automatic sharing without appropriate safeguards.

### 6. A reusable platform

Game-specific modules own gameplay while shared services handle gift creation, delivery, personalization contracts, status, entitlements and operations. Creative assets, character systems and effects should be reusable where helpful, **without flattening distinct game worlds into a single generic template**.

### 7. Commercial and ethical trust

Before a public paid launch, provide appropriate consent and privacy protections for children's information, production persistence, safe content, rights-cleared assets, secure delivery, support, payment integrity, accessibility and mobile reliability.

## What we must not accidentally build

- A one-game Dodo website with no credible path to a catalogue.
- A decorative birthday card presented as a playable game.
- A generic quiz factory where only names, colours or background images change.
- A game-engine authoring product aimed at professional developers instead of everyday gift-givers.
- A low-quality collection of unrelated games with inconsistent art.
- A technically extensive backend that arrives before there is a delightful, validated game-gifting experience.
- A growth system that pressures children to share or play compulsively.

## Decision rule

**Do not downgrade the vision to meet an arbitrary milestone, coding convenience or initial freelance budget.** Use focused experiments, existing tools and reusable assets to control cost without sacrificing the intended quality bar. Any material narrowing of product ambition requires explicit owner approval.

The owner may approve the sequence and scope of individual releases without conceding the long-term platform goal.
